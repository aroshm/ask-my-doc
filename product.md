# Nimbus 13 Laptop Line

Nimbus is a fictional laptop line used to practice building a grounded chatbot. It comes in two models: the Nimbus Pro 13 and the Nimbus Air 13.

## Models and pricing

- The Nimbus Pro 13 (product id: pro-13) has a launch price of $1,299.
- The Nimbus Air 13 (product id: air-13) has a launch price of $999.
- Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Prices can change, so live prices and stock come from the store system.

## Specifications

- Both models have a 13.3 inch display.
- The Pro 13 display is 2.8K resolution at 120 Hz. The Air 13 display is 1080p at 60 Hz.
- The Pro 13 has 16 GB of memory and 512 GB of storage. The Air 13 has 8 GB of memory and 256 GB of storage.
- Memory and storage are soldered and cannot be upgraded after purchase.
- The Air 13 has the longer battery life at up to 18 hours of video playback. The Pro 13 lasts up to 14 hours.
- The Pro 13 weighs 1.4 kg. The Air 13 weighs 1.1 kg.
- Both models have two USB-C ports with Thunderbolt support and one headphone jack.
- Only the Pro 13 has an HDMI port.
- Both models support Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.3.

## Colors and accessories

- Both models are available in two colors: Silver and Graphite.
- A 65 W USB-C charger is included in the box with both models.
- A carrying sleeve is sold separately for $39.

## Returns

- Unopened and opened laptops can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- The laptop must be returned with all original accessories.
